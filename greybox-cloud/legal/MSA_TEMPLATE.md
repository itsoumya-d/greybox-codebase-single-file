# Greybox Master Services Agreement Template

Proprietary and confidential. Copyright (c) 2026 Greybox Studio.

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA") governs Greybox Studio services ordered
by the customer identified in an order form.

## 1. Services

Greybox provides AI-assisted game design tooling, managed inference, engine
plugins, collaboration, Pro modules, marketplace access, playtest automation,
enterprise administration, and related support. Specific entitlements, seats,
usage, regions, service levels, and fees are set in the order form.

## 2. Customer Data And Game IP

Customer owns its game concepts, designs, source assets, prompts, artifacts,
and shipped-game IP. Greybox receives only the rights needed to operate,
secure, support, and improve the service for that customer. Greybox will not
sell customer game IP and will not train models on customer data without a
separate explicit opt-in.

## 3. Greybox Technology

Greybox owns the service, cloud systems, engine plugins, Pro modules,
marketplace infrastructure, playtest agents, software, models, know-how, and
improvements. Apache-2.0 open-core components remain governed by their open
source license. Closed-core modules remain proprietary.

## 4. AI-Assisted Output

Greybox output is AI-assisted. Customer is responsible for reviewing game
artifacts, validating third-party rights, testing gameplay, complying with
platform requirements, and crediting human designers in shipped artifacts.

## 5. Fees And Billing

Customer will pay subscription, usage, overage, seat, plugin, module, and
marketplace fees in the order form. Managed inference usage is metered. Late
amounts may be suspended after notice. Taxes are customer responsibility unless
Greybox is required to collect them.

## 6. Security And Compliance

Greybox will maintain commercially reasonable security controls, audit logs,
SCIM/SSO where purchased, and data-residency controls described in
`legal/DATA_RESIDENCY.md`. The DPA governs personal data processing.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's non-public business, technical, product,
security, pricing, and customer information using reasonable care and only use
it to perform under this MSA.

## 8. Support And Availability

Support level, response targets, and uptime commitments are stated in the order
form. Alpha, beta, free, marketplace creator, and preview features may be
changed or discontinued and are excluded from service credits unless stated.

## 9. Marketplace And Third-Party Content

Marketplace creators own their listings unless otherwise stated. Greybox may
remove illegal, infringing, unsafe, deceptive, or policy-violating content.
Customer is responsible for evaluating third-party marketplace content before
using it in shipped games.

## 10. Term And Termination

Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after 30 days' written
notice. Greybox may suspend access for security risk, non-payment, unlawful use,
or platform abuse. On termination, data return/deletion follows the DPA.

## 11. Warranties And Disclaimers

Greybox will provide services in a professional manner. Except as stated in this
MSA or an order form, services are provided "as is" to the maximum extent
permitted by law, with no warranty that outputs are error-free or suitable for
commercial release without human review.

## 12. Liability

Except for excluded claims, each party's aggregate liability is capped at fees
paid or payable in the 12 months before the event giving rise to liability.
Excluded claims should be specified in the order form and may include payment
obligations, confidentiality breach, IP misuse, and data protection violations.
