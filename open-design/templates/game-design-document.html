Executive Summary

Design Pillars

  • Readable skill-first combat with tactical positioning.
  • Exploration that turns world knowledge into power.
  • Progression that unlocks new decisions, not only higher stats.

Target Player

Players who enjoy mastery, build expression, memorable bosses, and handcrafted world secrets.

Unique Hook

Faction reputation changes enemy behavior and dungeon generation across the campaign.

Scope Guardrail

Vertical slice includes one hub, one dungeon biome, three enemy roles, one boss, and one progression branch.

Game Overview

FieldDefinition
Core LoopScout room, choose approach, fight or bypass, claim reward, upgrade build, descend.
Meta LoopRecover in hub, alter faction standing, unlock gear, open new routes.
Fail StateLose carried loot, keep discovered map knowledge and faction memory.
Win StateDefeat the biome boss and claim a campaign relic.

Mechanics

SystemPlayer DecisionTuning Notes
CombatCommit, dodge, parry, stagger, spend stamina.Define startup, active, recovery, hitstop, and stamina costs.
ExplorationChoose main path, secret route, or extraction.Use landmarks, locked gates, and risk-reward branches.
ProgressionInvest in weapon, spell, faction, or survival branch.No dead levels; each tier unlocks a behavior.
EconomySpend gold on upgrades, repairs, rumors, or faction favors.Track sources, sinks, caps, and hoarding risk.

Art & Audio Direction

Art Bible

Fantasy RPG with parchment menus, bronze borders, readable magic schools, and faction seals.

HUD Rules

Health, stamina, skill slots, status effects, quest hint, and boss bar stay stable during combat.

Audio States

Explore, combat-light, combat-heavy, boss, safe hub, rare loot, low health.

Accessibility

Subtitles, reduced flash, reduced shake, remapping, timing assist, HUD scale, colorblind-safe status icons.
