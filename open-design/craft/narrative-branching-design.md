# Narrative Branching Design

Use when designing dialogue trees, quests, factions, companion reactions, lore, and choice consequences.

## Principles

- Choices should reveal values, relationships, resources, or risk.
- Branches need state variables, consequences, merge points, and production cost labels.
- Failure-forward paths keep the story moving while changing opportunity, trust, resources, or danger.
- Dialogue options should express player intent, not just paraphrase NPC exposition.
- Lore should be discoverable through action, place, objects, and consequence.

## Checklist

- Every branch has a variable, consequence, and merge or terminal condition.
- Faction and companion reactions follow stated motives.
- Skill checks have success, fail, and partial outcomes.
- Expensive content branches are marked for production review.
- The quest remains playable when a player refuses, fails, or lacks an item.
