# Quest Structure Systems

Quest design connects player action, world consequence, reward, and narrative pacing.

## What To Produce

- Quest goal, giver or trigger, objective chain, branching conditions, failure states, rewards, and world-state changes.
- A mission-type mix: exploration, combat, stealth, puzzle, escort, survival, boss hunt, social, or crafting.
- Dependency notes for level layout, dialogue, cinematics, economy, and telemetry.

## Design Rules

- Each objective should change what the player knows, owns, unlocks, or believes.
- Branches need meaningful consequence and production merge points.
- Fail-forward outcomes should preserve pacing when strict failure would frustrate.
- Rewards must match effort, risk, and narrative importance.
- Quest variety should rotate verbs, location pressure, social stakes, and reward types.

## Production Gates

- Budget dialogue lines, cinematic beats, bespoke props, encounter scripting, and localization.
- Track telemetry: abandon rate, objective confusion, retry count, branch selection, and reward satisfaction.
- Provide a reusable quest template before designing many bespoke missions.

## Agent Checklist

- Include objective chain and branch logic.
- Include rewards and world-state consequences.
- Include production dependencies.
- Include player-agency and accessibility notes.
