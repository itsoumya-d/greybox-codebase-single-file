# Encounter Design

Encounters are teachable pressure tests for the game's mechanics, not random enemy piles.

## What To Produce

- Encounter goal, arena shape, enemy composition, spawn timing, recovery windows, and reward.
- A mechanic-introduction plan: teach, combine, pressure, then release.
- Failure and retry notes that explain how the player learns without feeling cheated.

## Design Rules

- Every enemy role needs a readable job: pressure, flank, anchor, punish, protect, disrupt, or teach.
- Escalation should add one major variable at a time unless the encounter is an intentional climax.
- Recovery windows are part of the design; players need moments to heal, reload, reposition, or parse the arena.
- Reward placement should pull the player through the space and reinforce the objective.
- Optional difficulty modifiers should add mastery, not mandatory grind.

## Production Gates

- Keep spawn count, AI variety, navigation needs, and VFX density within the target engine and device budget.
- Mark required telemetry: deaths by phase, time to first hit, retry count, resource drain, and exit health.
- Provide a greybox version before final art dependencies are required.

## Agent Checklist

- Define player objective and success state.
- Define enemy roles and counterplay.
- Define arena readability.
- Define tuning variables and playtest metrics.
