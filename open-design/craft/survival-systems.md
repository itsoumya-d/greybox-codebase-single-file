# Survival Systems

Survival pressure should create tension, planning, and relief without becoming accounting busywork.

## What To Produce

- Needs model, resource loop, crafting loop, shelter rules, environmental hazards, inventory pressure, and extraction or recovery options.
- Risk/reward structure for scarcity, exploration, weather, enemies, durability, infection, hunger, thirst, heat, cold, or oxygen.
- Tuning notes for depletion rates, resource spawn, carrying capacity, and fail states.

## Design Rules

- Survival meters must support decisions, not merely punish time spent playing.
- Scarcity needs readable causes and counterplay.
- Safe zones, shelters, campfires, or extraction points create emotional release.
- Crafting recipes should reinforce exploration routes and biome identity.
- Inventory pressure should force meaningful priorities, not constant cleanup.

## Production Gates

- Budget item count, recipes, status effects, environmental simulations, UI complexity, and save-state edge cases.
- Track telemetry: starvation deaths, abandoned sessions, hoarding, unused recipes, and extraction success rate.
- Provide accessibility assists for timing pressure and cognitive load.

## Agent Checklist

- Include pressure sources and relief valves.
- Include crafting and inventory constraints.
- Include tuning variables.
- Include frustration and accessibility safeguards.
