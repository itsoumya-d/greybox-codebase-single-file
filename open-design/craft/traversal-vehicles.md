# Traversal And Vehicles

Traversal systems sell freedom through control, readability, and world fit.

## What To Produce

- Movement verbs, vehicle roles, control model, camera behavior, speed bands, collision rules, and upgrade hooks.
- Route design for walking, climbing, grappling, swimming, flying, driving, riding, or zero-gravity movement.
- Risk/reward rules for shortcuts, hazards, fuel, durability, stealth exposure, or combat vulnerability.

## Design Rules

- Each traversal verb needs a clear fantasy and a clear limitation.
- Camera and collision must stay readable at top speed.
- Vehicles should change route choices, encounter shape, and reward placement.
- Skill expression can come from timing, route choice, physics mastery, or resource management.
- Fast traversal needs world scale, signage, and minimap support.

## Production Gates

- Budget physics, animation, camera, networking, collision, sound, VFX, and level-design support.
- Provide playable-concept controls before building content around the vehicle.
- Test motion sickness, input remapping, and reduced camera shake options.

## Agent Checklist

- Include control and camera rules.
- Include route and encounter impact.
- Include vehicle tuning variables.
- Include platform and accessibility constraints.
