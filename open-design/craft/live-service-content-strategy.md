# Live Service Content Strategy

Live service design must create reasons to return without burning out players or the team.

## What To Produce

- Seasonal fantasy, player promise, event cadence, reward track, content rotation, and patch rhythm.
- Retention goals tied to healthy engagement, not coercion.
- Community beats: preview, launch, mid-season refresh, finale, and post-season archive.

## Design Rules

- Seasonal mechanics should refresh the core loop rather than replace it.
- Rewards should favor trust: cosmetics, expression, convenience, and fair catch-up paths.
- Time-limited content needs clear communication and respectful fallback access when possible.
- Meta shifts should create new mastery without invalidating favorite builds.
- Community events should encourage cooperation without requiring social pressure.

## Production Gates

- Budget events by content load, QA load, localization, backend operations, analytics, and player/community support.
- Define rollback plans for economy, matchmaking, rewards, and progression bugs.
- Avoid cadence promises that exceed team capacity.

## Agent Checklist

- Include season cadence and event lifecycle.
- Include ethical monetization notes.
- Include telemetry and burnout signals.
- Include production workload and maintenance risks.
