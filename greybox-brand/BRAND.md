# Greybox Brand Guidelines

Greybox is the AI-assisted design layer for shipped games. The system should
feel confident, practical, and designer-first: speak in pillars, loops, feel,
readability, pacing, vertical slices, and engine handoff rather than generic AI
automation language.

## Voice

- Confident, not boastful.
- Verb-led headlines.
- Designer-first vocabulary before model vocabulary.
- Always say "AI-assisted" unless a specific artifact is fully generated and
  context is provided.
- Use Greybox as a product name or adjective, never as a verb or generic noun.
  Say "block out a level with Greybox" or "AI-assisted blockout" instead of
  turning the product name into an action.
- Credit the human designer in shipped artifacts:
  `<meta name="generator" content="Greybox + <designer-name>">`.

## Logo Usage

- Minimum mark size: 16px.
- Clear-space: at least one full mark height on every side.
- Never recolor the Spark Orange play triangle.
- Never separate the mark from the wordmark inside lockups.
- Never apply shadows, blurs, bevels, gradients, outlines, or other effects to
  the mark.
- Use the mono mark only where color reproduction is impossible.
- Use the inverse mark only on dark graphite or ink backgrounds.

## Color

Graphite, Paper, and Ink are the base surfaces. Spark Orange is the action and
play signal. Semantic colors are reserved for loop state, warnings, critical
issues, and collaborative AI focus.

## Typography

Use Inter Tight 700 for the lowercase wordmark. Product UI uses Inter for body
copy and JetBrains Mono for code, diffs, engine paths, and telemetry.
